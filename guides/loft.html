Loft conversion ranges

Last checked September 2026 · Compiled UK mid bands, before regional factor

A rooflight conversion and a rear dormer are not the same job. Compiled 2026 bands before VAT, region and contingency:

  • Velux / rooflight: £22,000–£50,000 (mid £34,000)
  • Rear dormer: £35,000–£75,000 (mid £50,000)

Treat the mid point as a planning number. Collect written quotes on the same drawing.
