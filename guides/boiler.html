Combi swap versus conversion

Last checked September 2026

A like-for-like combi in the same cupboard is the cheap job in 2026 guides: compiled band £1,800–£4,500, mid £2,800, before VAT and contingency. Converting a regular or system setup is a different job. This lab only models the like-for-like swap.
