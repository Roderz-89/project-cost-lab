Advertising disclosure

Last updated 3 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

This site is parked. Project Cost Lab does not currently display third-party advertisements. Ads are off. Ad scripts are not loaded, and the host is not offered as advertising inventory. The lab stays parked until that position is changed on purpose. Nothing on this page turns ads back on.

ads.txt lists no sellers

ads.txt lists no authorised sellers while the lab stays parked. The file at /ads.txt is a short note that this host does not authorise digital sellers. It does not name an advertising partner as allowed to sell this site. Do not treat projectcostlab.co.uk as an inventory site, and do not read the file as permission to place ads. If seller lines were ever added, this page would have to say that the file had changed. It has not.

What “ads off” means on the pages

Guides, the calculator, and the policy pages are informational. You should not see an advertisement labelled as such, and you should not see an unlabelled unit styled to look like one. There is no ad slot standing in for a cost range. The low, mid, and high figures come from the calculator’s own illustration, not from a sponsor. A paragraph about boilers, lofts, or extensions is editorial. It is not a paid placement, and a contractor is not buying position on the page.

While parked, advertising cookies are not used. The cookies page says the same thing. We do not show a consent banner for advertising cookies, because those cookies are not in use. Browser controls remain available if you want to block site data anyway.

How ads would be labelled if they returned

This section describes a standard we would apply if ads were switched on later. It does not mean they are on. If advertising returned, units would be labelled, for example “Advertisement”. On calculator and guide pages they would sit after the explanation of the range, not in place of the range, and they would not be styled as a contractor price or a tender figure. We would not put ads on a page that was only a number with no context. The mid figure would remain a planning band, not a quote, whether or not an ad sat further down the page.

That future standard is not an invitation to a network, and it is not a claim that a review is underway. The site is noindex while parked. Policy pages keep saying ads are off so the live host and this disclosure match.

Independence of the ranges

Compiled cost bands are editorial illustrations from Rodway Labs. Advertisers do not set our mid points, regional factors, access factors, or VAT display options, and none are set by an advertiser today because ads are off. We are not a contractor, architect, or quantity surveyor. A band is not a price, and proximity to a policy page about advertising does not make it one.

Regional factors, the access settings, the listed-building toggle, contingency, and optional allowances such as scaffold or skips are explained in the guides and limited by the disclaimer. They are planning tools. They are not offers, and they are not influenced by a seller line — there is no seller line.

What to do if you see something that looks like an ad

If a page on this host appears to load a third-party advertising unit, or if a copy of ads.txt elsewhere claims this site authorises a seller, treat that as a fault and email hello@projectcostlab.co.uk. Tell us the exact URL. We will check the parked deployment. Do not click through an unexpected unit and assume Rodway Labs endorsed the destination. The privacy notice describes the parked position alongside this page.

Questions about a cost band still belong with the calculator and with written quotes from people who will do the work. This inbox will not price a job because an advertising question was attached to it. The VAT toggle remains a display choice, not a ruling.
