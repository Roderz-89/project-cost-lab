Advertising disclosure

Last updated 10 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

This site does not show ads. Project Cost Lab does not display third-party advertisements. Ad scripts are not loaded, and the host is not offered as advertising inventory. The privacy dialog records a choice only; it does not load an ad.

ads.txt lists no sellers

This site does not show ads. The file at /ads.txt is a short note that this host does not authorise digital sellers. It does not name an advertising partner. Do not treat projectcostlab.co.uk as an inventory site, and do not read the file as permission to place ads.

What this means on the pages

Guides, the calculator, and the policy pages are informational. You should not see an advertisement labelled as such, and you should not see an unlabelled unit styled to look like one. There is no ad slot standing in for a cost range. The low, mid, and high figures come from the calculator’s own illustration, not from a sponsor. A paragraph about boilers, lofts, or extensions is editorial. It is not a paid placement, and a contractor is not buying position on the page.

Advertising cookies are not used. The cookies page describes the banner: Accept all and Essential only, stored in localStorage, with neither choice loading an ad or analytics script. Browser controls remain available if you want to clear that stored choice.

Independence of the ranges

Compiled cost bands are editorial illustrations from Rodway Labs. Advertisers do not set our mid points, regional factors, access factors, or VAT display options, and none are set by an advertiser because this site does not show ads. We are not a contractor, architect, or quantity surveyor. A band is not a price, and proximity to a policy page about advertising does not make it one.

Regional factors, the access settings, the listed-building toggle, contingency, and optional allowances such as scaffold or skips are explained in the guides and limited by the disclaimer. They are planning tools. They are not offers, and they are not influenced by a seller line — there is no seller line.

What to do if you see something that looks like an ad

If a page on this host appears to load a third-party advertising unit, or if a copy of ads.txt elsewhere claims this site authorises a seller, treat that as a fault and email hello@projectcostlab.co.uk. Tell us the exact URL. We will check the deployment. Do not click through an unexpected unit and assume Rodway Labs endorsed the destination.
