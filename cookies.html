Cookies

Last updated 3 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

This page describes cookies and similar technologies on Project Cost Lab, published by Rodway Labs in the United Kingdom. The site is parked. Advertising cookies are not used while this site is parked. There is no advertising cookie banner on these pages, and this notice does not describe a banner you can open, because none is shown while ads are off.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site can store on your device. Similar technologies include local storage in the browser and pixels in a page. Some are essential to deliver a page over HTTPS. Others, on sites that run advertising or measurement products, remember a browser between visits so an ad network or an analytics tool can recognise it. Project Cost Lab explains that split so you can see what this parked host is and is not doing.

What this parked site uses

  • Essential / security — technologies needed to serve the site over HTTPS and to protect the host against abuse, including those used by the content delivery network that fronts projectcostlab.co.uk. These are about delivering the page, not about building a marketing profile.
  • Advertising — not used while the site is parked. Ads are off. The site does not load an ad script, and it does not ask an advertising partner to set a cookie. If that ever changed, the advertising page and this page would have to say so first. They do not say so today.
  • Analytics / measurement — the calculator does not send your inputs to Rodway Labs as a job enquiry. We do not describe a marketing analytics product on this parked host. Any aggregate operational logs a host needs in order to stay online are not a record of the cost band you built.

The calculator stays in the browser

When you change region, specification, access, VAT display, or contingency, the band is calculated in your browser so the page can show a low, mid, and high figure. Those inputs are for an illustration. They are not a quote, they are not stored by us as a project file, and they are not an instruction to a contractor. Closing the page discards the illustration unless you copied the range yourself. Copying a range puts text on your clipboard; it does not open an account and it does not email Rodway Labs.

Do not type personal financial information into the calculator. It does not ask for your name, your address, or a quote you received. If a future page ever asked for that, it would be a different service and this notice would be rewritten. It does not ask today.

Your choices, without a banner

Because advertising cookies are not in use, we do not show a consent banner and we do not store a record of an advertising choice. Browser settings still let you block or delete cookies and site data for this host if you want to. Blocking cookies does not remove the informational pages: the guides and the calculator are ordinary HTML and script served to the browser. You do not need to accept an ad partner to read a band.

Industry opt-out pages run by advertising networks are relevant on sites that personalise ads. They are not a control for Project Cost Lab while it is parked, because this host is not asking those networks to recognise you. If you arrived here from another site that does run ads, use that site’s own settings for its cookies. Nothing you select elsewhere changes the fact that this parked lab is not placing advertising cookies.

What we will not do with this page

This notice is not a contract to build, and it is not a promise that a cost band is accurate for your house. Cookies policy and the calculator’s figures are separate. A mid figure remains a planning band whether or not your browser stores a cookie. Rodway Labs is not a contractor, architect, or quantity surveyor. The VAT toggle is a display choice, not a ruling.
