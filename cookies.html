Cookies

Last checked 14 Oct 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Project Cost Lab (projectcostlab.co.uk), which is run by Rodway Labs in the United Kingdom. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), a site must ask before storing information on your device unless that storage is strictly necessary. Here is exactly what this site does.

What this site stores

Only one thing: your privacy choice.

NameTypePurposeKept for
pcl-consentlocalStorage (first party)Remembers whether you chose all (Accept All) or essential (Essential only), so we don’t ask again on every page. Not used for tracking, and not sent to us.Until you clear site data or change your choice

Nothing is stored until you make a choice. If you close the dialog without choosing, the visit is treated as essential only.

What this site does not use

  • No analytics. No analytics or measurement tool runs on this site.
  • No advertising cookies. This site does not show ads, and no advertising script is loaded, even if you choose Accept All. ads.txt authorises no ad seller.
  • No third-party tags. The calculator runs in your browser and your inputs are not sent to us.

The privacy dialog

On your first visit, a “We value your privacy” dialog asks for your choice.

  • Accept All stores pcl-consent=all.
  • Options shows the purposes in use and lets you pick Essential only (stores pcl-consent=essential) or Accept All, then save.

The only purpose listed is “Store and/or access information on a device”, used strictly to remember your choice. No advertising or analytics purposes are active, so today both choices have the same effect: they record your choice and close the dialog.

Advertising

This site does not show ads.

Hosting

The site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your browser’s requests, and may keep basic logs of them, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. That is part of hosting. We do not use it for tracking or advertising. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy describes its processing.

Security check by the host. Cloudflare may add a small security script, loaded from /cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/, to detect bots, and may set a cf_clearance cookie recording that the check passed. This is set by the host for security. It is not analytics or advertising, and Project Cost Lab does not use it to track you. See Cloudflare’s cookie list.

How to change your choice

  • Use the Privacy preferences link at the foot of every page to reopen the dialog.
  • Or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. That removes pcl-consent, and the dialog will appear again.
