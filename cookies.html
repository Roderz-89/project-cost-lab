Cookies

Last checked 15 Nov 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Project Cost Lab (projectcostlab.co.uk), which is run by Rodway Labs in the United Kingdom. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), a site must ask before storing or reading information on your device unless that is strictly necessary. Here is exactly what this site does.

What this site stores

Nothing. Project Cost Lab sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser’s local storage, so there is no consent banner to click through.

Analytics without cookies

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not set cookies or use local storage and does not identify you; we see totals such as page views per page.

Advertising

This site does not show ads. No advertising script is loaded. ads.txt authorises no ad seller.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your browser’s requests, and may keep basic logs of them, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. That is part of hosting. We do not use it for tracking or advertising. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy describes its processing.

Security check by the host. Cloudflare may add a small security script, loaded from /cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/, to detect bots, and may set a cf_clearance cookie recording that the check passed. This is strictly necessary for security. It is not analytics or advertising, and Project Cost Lab does not use it to track you. See Cloudflare’s cookie list.

Earlier versions of this site

Until November 2026 the site showed a privacy dialog that saved your choice in local storage as pcl-consent. That dialog has been removed. If the value is still in your browser it does nothing; you can clear it by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Questions

To ask about this page, use the Contact page. See also Privacy and Advertising.
