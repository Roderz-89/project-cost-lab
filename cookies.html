Cookies

Last updated 3 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

This page describes cookies and similar technologies on Project Cost Lab, published by Rodway Labs in the United Kingdom. Ads are off. A first-party banner at the bottom of each page asks you to choose Accept all or Essential only. That choice is stored in this browser’s localStorage under the key pcl-consent, as all or essential. If you dismiss nothing, the visit stays essential only: we do not write pcl-consent until you choose. Neither choice loads an advertising script, an analytics script, or an advertising cookie while ads are off. There is no active publisher ID on this host.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site can store on your device. Similar technologies include local storage in the browser and pixels in a page. Some are essential to deliver a page over HTTPS. Others, on sites that run advertising or measurement products, remember a browser between visits so an ad network or an analytics tool can recognise it. Project Cost Lab explains that split so you can see what this host is and is not doing while ads are off.

What this site uses

  • Essential / security — technologies needed to serve the site over HTTPS and to protect the host against abuse, including those used by the content delivery network that fronts projectcostlab.co.uk. These are about delivering the page, not about building a marketing profile.
  • Advertising — not used. Ads are off. The site does not load an ad script, and it does not ask an advertising partner to set a cookie, including after Accept all. If that ever changed, the advertising page and this page would have to say so first. They do not say so today. ads.txt authorises no seller.
  • Analytics / measurement — not loaded. The calculator does not send your inputs to Rodway Labs as a job enquiry. Accept all does not start a measurement product. Any aggregate operational logs a host needs in order to stay online are not a record of the cost band you built.
  • Your cookie choice — only if you press a banner button. The value lives in localStorage on your device, not in an advertising cookie. Clearing site data for this host removes it, and the banner shows again.

The calculator stays in the browser

When you change region, specification, access, VAT display, or contingency, the band is calculated in your browser so the page can show a low, mid, and high figure. Those inputs are for an illustration. They are not a quote, they are not stored by us as a project file, and they are not an instruction to a contractor. Closing the page discards the illustration unless you copied the range yourself. Copying a range puts text on your clipboard; it does not open an account and it does not email Rodway Labs.

Do not type personal financial information into the calculator. It does not ask for your name, your address, or a quote you received. If a future page ever asked for that, it would be a different service and this notice would be rewritten. It does not ask today.

The banner

The banner is part of the page (a region labelled “Cookie choices”) until you choose. Essential only stores pcl-consent=essential. Accept all stores pcl-consent=all. Both buttons do the same thing today: they record the choice and hide the banner. They do not set advertising cookies and they do not load Google AdSense, adsbygoogle, or any other third-party tag. Accept all is recorded so a later, deliberate switch-on of ads would have a stored choice to respect. It does not turn ads on by itself.

Browser settings still let you block or delete cookies and site data for this host. Blocking or clearing site data removes the stored choice and brings the banner back. It does not remove the informational pages: the guides and the calculator are ordinary HTML and script served to the browser. You do not need to accept an ad partner to read a band.

Industry opt-out pages run by advertising networks are relevant on sites that personalise ads. They are not a control for Project Cost Lab while ads are off, because this host is not asking those networks to recognise you. If you arrived here from another site that does run ads, use that site’s own settings for its cookies. Nothing you select elsewhere changes the fact that this lab is not placing advertising cookies.

What we will not do with this page

This notice is not a contract to build, and it is not a promise that a cost band is accurate for your house. Cookies policy and the calculator’s figures are separate. A mid figure remains a planning band whether or not your browser stores a cookie. Rodway Labs is not a contractor, architect, or quantity surveyor. The VAT toggle is a display choice, not a ruling.
