Cookies

Last updated 27 September 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

This page describes how cookies and similar technologies may be used on Project Cost Lab.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. Some are essential to deliver the page; others help with advertising or measurement.

Categories we may use

  • Essential / security — needed to serve the site over HTTPS, balance traffic or protect against abuse (including technologies used by our CDN).
  • Advertising — Google AdSense and partners may set cookies to show ads, limit how often you see an ad, and (where allowed) personalise ads. See Advertising.
  • Analytics / measurement — aggregate statistics about visits and page performance so we can improve guides and the calculator layout.

The calculator

Calculator inputs are handled in the browser for illustrating a cost band. They are not a quote and are not submitted to Rodway Labs as a job enquiry.

Your choices

Browser settings usually let you block or delete cookies. Where a consent banner is shown, you can also change non-essential choices there. Blocking advertising cookies typically reduces personalisation; the informational tools remain available.

Google ad settings and industry opt-out tools (where available in your region) provide further controls over personalised ads.
