Contact

Publisher: Rodway Labs, United Kingdom. Email: hello@projectcostlab.co.uk.

Email us about a range, a correction or a broken control — not for a quote on your house. This site does not show ads.

What to send

Say which page you were on, which project you picked, and which settings you changed (region, spec, access, living in, listed, VAT, contingency). For a correction, quote the sentence and what it should say. For a bug, name the browser.

What we can’t do

We can’t price a job from drawings or photos, say whether you need planning permission, Building Regulations approval or a Party Wall notice, recommend a contractor, judge a quote, or decide your VAT. Please don’t send bank details or other personal financial information. Rodway Labs is not a contractor, architect or quantity surveyor.

What to expect

We aim to reply within a few working days. Where the calculator and a guide disagree, we fix the page. For a number you can act on, get written quotes on the same drawing; read the disclaimer and guides first.
