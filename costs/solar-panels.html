What moves the price

  • Scaffolding. A large fixed cost that most roofs need; tick the scaffolding extra to include it. It is one reason small arrays cost more per kWp.
  • Panel and inverter choice. Higher-efficiency panels and hybrid inverters (ready for a battery) cost more.
  • The roof. Slate, multiple roof faces and in-roof mounting add labour.
  • Electrics. An old consumer unit may need replacing to connect the system.

Planning, Building Regulations and permissions

Roof-mounted solar panels on most houses in England are permitted development, subject to conditions on how far they project and on listed buildings and conservation areas. Your installer will notify the electricity network operator. To be paid for exported electricity through the Smart Export Guarantee, suppliers usually require an MCS certified installation. Check the current rules on the Planning Portal before you commit.

General information for England unless stated, checked 15 November 2026. Rules differ in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland and change over time — confirm with your council, Building Control or the Planning Portal before you start.

Solar panels (PV) cost FAQ

What size solar system do I need?

It depends on your roof and electricity use. Around 4 kWp is a common size for a three-bedroom house with a suitable roof; use the size setting to compare.

Does the band include a battery?

No. Home battery storage has its own project in the calculator, priced per kWh.

Is VAT charged on solar panels?

Installing them in a home is 0% VAT until 31 March 2027, then 5% from 1 April 2027. Panels bought without installation are standard-rated.
