Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · Operator: Rodway Labs

Project Cost Lab (projectcostlab.co.uk) is run by Rodway Labs in the United Kingdom. This notice explains, in plain English, what personal data is involved when you use the site. We aim to follow the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Who we are

Rodway Labs operates this site and is responsible for the choices described here. To contact us about privacy, use the Contact page.

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and the site does not collect your name, address or payment details.
  • The calculator runs in your browser. Your inputs are not sent to us.
  • The only thing we store on your device is your privacy choice, saved in localStorage as pcl-consent.
  • We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising cookies.
  • This site does not show ads.

What is stored on your device

When you make a choice in the “We value your privacy” dialog, your browser stores one value in localStorage under the key pcl-consent: either all (Accept All) or essential (Essential only). It stays on your device, it is not sent to us, and it is not used to track you. It is there so we don’t ask again on every page. Storing it is strictly necessary to remember the choice you asked us to remember. If you close the dialog without choosing, nothing is stored and we treat the visit as essential only.

Full details are on the Cookies page.

Analytics

We do not run any analytics or measurement tool on this site. Choosing Accept All does not start one.

Advertising

This site does not show ads. No advertising script is loaded, and no advertising cookies are set, whatever you choose. ads.txt authorises no ad seller. See also our Advertising page.

Hosting

The site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. As with any website, delivering a page means your browser’s request, including your IP address and basic technical details such as the browser type and the page requested, passes through Cloudflare’s network. Cloudflare may keep basic logs of those requests to run the service and protect it against abuse. We do not use that information to build a profile of you. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy describes its processing.

If you contact us

If you get in touch through the Contact page, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to fix anything you have reported. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy preferences link at the foot of every page. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings. That removes the stored choice, and the dialog will appear again on your next visit.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have rights to ask for access to personal data we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it, or to object to how it is used. Because this site collects so little, in most cases we will hold nothing beyond any message you have sent us. To make a request, use the Contact page. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Changes to this notice

We will update this page, with a new date, before any change to how the site uses cookies, storage or personal data.
