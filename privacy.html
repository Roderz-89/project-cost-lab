Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

Project Cost Lab is an informational UK cost-tools site. This notice explains what we (and our service providers) may process when you use the site.

What the calculator does

The cost-range calculator runs in your browser. Project choices and inputs are used locally to illustrate a band. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not use the calculator to collect your name, address or payment details.

Advertising

This site is parked. We do not currently use Google AdSense or other third-party ad networks on Project Cost Lab. There is no active publisher ID declared for this host, and ads.txt lists no authorised sellers.

If advertising is re-enabled later, AdSense (or similar) partners may use cookies or similar technologies, with units placed after explanatory content — never dressed as a contractor price or tender figure. See our Advertising page.

Cookies

Essential cookies (if any) keep the site working. Advertising and measurement cookies are described on our Cookies page. You can usually control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may limit ad personalisation rather than the calculator itself.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious or standard web analytics (including tools provided alongside Cloudflare) to understand aggregate traffic — for example which guides are read and whether pages load correctly. We use this to improve content, not to build a quote for your property.

Hosting and security

The site is served via Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process connection data (such as IP address and request metadata) to deliver content, apply HTTPS and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare’s published privacy documentation for details of that processing.
