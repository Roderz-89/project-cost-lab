Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026 · Publisher: Rodway Labs

Project Cost Lab is an informational UK cost-tools site. This notice explains what we (and our service providers) may process when you use the site.

What the calculator does

The cost-range calculator runs in your browser. Project choices and inputs are used locally to illustrate a band. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not use the calculator to collect your name, address or payment details.

Advertising

Ads are off. We do not currently use Google AdSense or other third-party ad networks on Project Cost Lab. There is no active publisher ID on this host, and ads.txt authorises no sellers. The pages do not load adsbygoogle or any other ad script.

If advertising is re-enabled later, on purpose, AdSense (or similar) partners may use cookies or similar technologies, with units placed after explanatory content — never dressed as a contractor price or tender figure. That has not happened. See our Advertising page.

Cookies and the consent banner

A banner on every page offers two equal choices: Accept all and Essential only. The choice is stored in localStorage as pcl-consent (all or essential). If you do not choose, we do not treat the visit as permission for advertising cookies. Essential only is the default. Neither button sets an advertising cookie or loads an ad or analytics script while ads are off. Details are on the Cookies page. You can clear the stored choice in your browser’s site data.

Essential cookies, if any, are those the host needs to serve the site over HTTPS and to protect it. They are not a marketing profile.

Analytics

We do not load a marketing analytics script on these pages, and Accept all does not start one. The calculator stays in your browser. Cloudflare, which serves the site, may process connection data as described below. That is not a quote for your property and it is not an advertising cookie.

Hosting and security

The site is served via Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process connection data (such as IP address and request metadata) to deliver content, apply HTTPS and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare’s published privacy documentation for details of that processing.
