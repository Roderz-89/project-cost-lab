About this section

News sits alongside our guides and the calculator. Guides explain how a project is priced; news posts cover changes as they are published, such as a new statistics release, a grant change or a VAT date. Posts are tagged Building costs, Grants & VAT, Planning or Energy. We do not recommend whether to start, delay or commission any work, or how to pay for it.

Where our figures come from

We use official and primary sources: the Office for National Statistics (construction output price indices), the Department for Business and Trade (building materials statistics), HMRC and HM Treasury (VAT and Budget measures), the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, GOV.UK and Ofgem (the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, energy schemes and the price cap), and the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government and the Planning Portal (planning, permitted development and Building Regulations). Every post lists its sources with links. Worked examples are run through the same calculator logic as the calculator, and we state the inputs so you can reproduce them. If an official figure has not been published yet, we say so rather than estimate it.

Who writes these posts

Posts are written and checked by Rodway Labs, the independent UK publisher behind Project Cost Lab. We are not a contractor, architect, quantity surveyor or adviser, and we do not take payment to feature any product, installer or scheme. Read more about us and our methodology.

How posts are updated

Each post shows when it was published and when it was last updated. When a post depends on a release that is still to come, it carries an update box with the expected date, and we update the post once the figures are out. Corrections are made in place and the “Last updated” date changes. If you spot an error, please contact us.
